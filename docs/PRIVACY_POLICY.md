# DuoDirector Privacy Policy

*Effective: November 25, 2026. Hosted at https://bryansnyder01-code.github.io/Duodirector/privacy/ (generated into site/privacy/index.html).*

DuoDirector is a video recording app. We built it so your recordings stay yours.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** DuoDirector has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. We don't receive your videos, audio, scripts or any information about you.

## What the app uses, and why
- **Camera and microphone:** only to record your videos and show live audio levels. Recording happens on your device.
- **Speech recognition:** only if you ask for captions. Speech is transcribed on your device, and DuoDirector never sends your audio to Apple or anyone else for this. If your iPhone can't transcribe a language on device, the app tells you instead of using a server.
- **Photos (add only):** only to save finished takes to your photo library when you ask. The app can't read your library.
- **iCloud (optional, off by default):** if you turn on sync, your project and, if you choose, your video files are stored in *your own* iCloud Drive. Apple's privacy policy covers this. We have no access.
- **Local network (optional):** Remote Producer connects only to a nearby iPhone or iPad that you approve. The connection is encrypted and peer to peer, and nothing passes through our servers.
- **Go Live (optional):** if you stream, your video and audio go directly to the streaming service you choose (for example YouTube or Twitch), using the stream key you enter. That service's privacy policy covers the stream. Your stream key is stored only in this device's Keychain.

## Children
DuoDirector isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
